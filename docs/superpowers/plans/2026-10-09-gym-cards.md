# Kanto Gym Challenge, Part 1: Cards and Decks — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Import the Scarlet & Violet 151 set, add its pack and a "Gym" deck format, and build the 15 Kanto leader decklists (8 Gym Leaders, the Elite Four, and 3 Champion lists) with every card they use made playable.

**Architecture:**

- **Cards:** 151 is one more set in the importer and `sets.json`, sold as a pack in a new `sv` era. Leader decks are JSON lists in `packages/cards/src/decks/gym/`, collected in `packages/cards/src/gym.ts`.
- **Format:** a pure `isGymLegal` / `validateGymDeck` pair in `packages/economy`, next to the Standard checks.
- **Engine:** two small, tested engine tasks add the few rules the 151 cards need that the engine lacks. Card scripts follow the existing one-script-per-card-name pattern.
- **Players:** nothing changes except a new "Scarlet & Violet" pack. The Gym Challenge screen, rewards and deck-builder format labels come in Part 2.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, React 19 (Shop and Binder only), TCGdex importer (`pnpm --filter @ptcg/cards import-cards`, run with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-kanto-gym-challenge-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Set:** `sv03.5` (Scarlet & Violet 151), 207 cards, regulation mark G, not Standard-legal. Images use the TCGdex pattern `https://assets.tcgdex.net/en/sv/sv03.5/<number>`.
- **Gym format (spec §4):**
  - the same rules as Standard: 60 cards, at most 4 copies of a name, at least 1 Basic Pokémon, at most 1 ACE SPEC;
  - every card must pass `isPlayable`;
  - a card is allowed if it is Standard-legal **or** its id starts with `sv03.5-`.
- **Card ids:** every leader deck uses the first printing (collector numbers 001–165) of each 151 card. Reprints share the script, because scripts are keyed by card name.
- **Trainer core (25 cards)**, the same as the theme decks: Lillie's Determination `me01-119` ×4, Iris's Fighting Spirit `sv09-149` ×2, Boss's Orders `me01-114` ×2, Ultra Ball `me01-131` ×4, Buddy-Buddy Poffin `me01-167` ×3, Mega Signal `me01-121` ×2, Switch `me01-130` ×2, Night Stretcher `me01-173` ×2, Wally's Compassion `me01-132` ×2, Air Balloon `me01-166` ×2.
  - **Stage 2 variant (26 cards):** replace the 2 Air Balloon with 3 Rare Candy `me01-125`.
  - **Leader Trainer swap:** where a deck lists "Trainer swap", replace the 2 Iris's Fighting Spirit with 2 of the named 151 Trainer.
- **Basic Energy ids:** Grass `mee-001`, Fire `mee-002`, Water `mee-003`, Lightning `mee-004`, Psychic `mee-005`, Fighting `mee-006`, Darkness `mee-007`.
- **Difficulty and win rates (spec §6):** the leader decks' target win rates are 30–50% (Brock, Misty, Surge, Erika), 40–60% (Koga, Sabrina, Blaine, Giovanni) and 45–65% (Elite Four, Champion). A deck outside its range has its counts adjusted, and the change is reported.
- **Playable means implemented.** A script is never a stub: the passive-hook shortcut in `isPlayable` may only be satisfied by a hook that really implements the card's Ability.
- **Existing scripts:** do not touch scripts that already exist. Mega Gengar ex (`me02-056`) is reused as is.
- **Commit trailers:** every commit ends with the attribution lines the session gives (the `Co-Authored-By` and `Claude-Session` lines).

### Changes from the spec (decided while planning, recorded in the spec too)

The planning pass read every 151 card's printed text against what the engine can run.

- **Dropped for now.** These need rules the engine doesn't have, and a script must implement the full text:
  - Kabuto, Kabutops, Omanyte, Omastar, Aerodactyl: they evolve from Fossil Trainers played as Pokémon.
  - Pidgeot and Seaking: they prevent damage **and effects**.
  - Seadra and Psyduck: they change coin-flip and attack rules.
  - Victreebel: it deals damage at the end of the opponent's turn.
- **Not used, though the spec named them:** Kangaskhan ex in Giovanni's deck and Rhydon, Gyarados and Exeggutor in Blue's. Each deck is trimmed to a coherent 4–6 Pokémon lines (and needs fewer card scripts).
- **Starmie's Mysterious Comet** discards the Pokémon itself, which is an engine item in Task 4.
- **Alakazam ex's Dimensional Hand** "can be used even if this Pokémon is on the Bench" is implemented as a normal attack from the Active Spot. Attacking from the Bench is out of scope.
- **Giovanni's deck is Darkness + Fighting**, since his Nido line is Darkness and Rhydon is Fighting.
- **Champion Blue** plays the Alakazam ex line plus one of three starter-line aces (Charizard ex, Blastoise ex or Venusaur ex), chosen against the challenger's deck in Part 2.
- **Script count:** about 80 distinct card names, not "50–70" (tally in Tasks 5–17).

## Review Focus

1. **New state fields reach the bots.** Anything new on a Pokémon slot or a player (marker kinds, `supporterPlayed`) must be copied by `viewFor` and by the Medium bot's determinizer; otherwise Medium plans on wrong state. Test: Task 3 (view and determinize round trip).
2. **Knockout hooks fire only for attack damage.** Machamp's Guts and Weezing's reaction must not trigger on Poison, Burn or a Knock Out from damage counters. Test: Task 4.
3. **Packs with an unknown rarity.** 151's "Hyper rare" (lower-case r) must not break opening, reveal tiers or the Shop; every pulled card exists. Test: Task 1.
4. **Gym legality edges.** A 151 card is Gym-legal but not Standard-legal; Standard Basic Energy and no-mark cards behave as before; a deck over 4 copies of a name or with no Basic fails in both formats. Test: Task 2.
5. **Self-removal can lose the game.** Starmie's Mysterious Comet must not be offered when Starmie is the only Pokémon in play; discarding an Active Pokémon with a Bench prompts a promotion. Test: Task 4 and Task 7.

## How the deck tasks work (Tasks 5–17)

Each deck task is one leader, in this order, and follows the same steps:

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** in `packages/cards/test/gym-<leader>.test.ts`, using `test/helpers.ts` and the `sv('NNN')` id helper added in Task 1:
  - one test per new script that asserts the behaviour and numbers listed in the task (every other attack and Ability follows its printed text in `cards.json`, and gets a test that asserts its damage or effect);
  - one deck test: the list totals 60, every card exists and passes `isPlayable`, and `validateGymDeck(deck, registry, ownAll)` returns `[]`.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run `npx vitest run --project cards packages/cards/test/gym-<leader>.test.ts`. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Add the scripts as `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv03.5/<NNN>.ts`, register each in `scripts/index.ts`, add `src/decks/gym/<leader>.json`, and add the deck to `GYM_DECKS` in `src/gym.ts`.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run `pnpm check`. Expected: green.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Commit `feat(cards): <Leader> gym deck`.

A card marked ⚙ needs an engine item from Task 3 or 4. Deck lists give `id ×count` (the 151 id `sv03.5-NNN` is written as NNN), and the Energy fills the deck to 60.

### Task 3: Engine A — timed effects, Resistance option, supporter memory

**Files:**

- Modify: `packages/engine/src/types.ts`, `effects.ts`, `combat.ts`, `energy.ts`, `trainers.ts`, `view.ts`, `index.ts`; `packages/bots/src/determinize.ts`
- Test: `packages/engine/test/gym-markers.test.ts`, `packages/bots/test/determinize.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**

- Produces:
  - `MarkerKind` gains `'increaseOutgoing' | 'preventDamage' | 'attackCostMore' | 'retreatCostMore'`. `Marker` is unchanged; `amount` is the size.
  - `EffectCtx.addMarker(ref, kind, amount = 0, turns = 1)`: `untilTurn = turn + turns`. "During your next turn" on your own Pokémon uses `turns = 2`. It also drops markers that have already expired from that slot (the deferred minor from the theme-deck review).
  - Damage pipeline (`dealAttackDamage`), in this order:
    1. the attacker's `increaseOutgoing` and `reduceOutgoing` markers are applied together before Weakness and Resistance;
    2. then the existing steps;
    3. then, unless `ignoreDefenderEffects`, the defender's `preventDamage` marker makes the damage 0.
  - Attack cost: each active `attackCostMore` marker on the attacking slot adds one {C} to every attack's cost (`canUseAttack` and the cost shown in legal actions use one function).
  - Retreat cost: each active `retreatCostMore` marker adds 1 in `getRetreatCost`.
  - `AttackScript.damage` may return `ignoreResistance: true` (Weakness still applies); `dealAttackDamage` takes the same option.
  - `PlayerState.supporterPlayed?: { turn: number; name: string } | null`, set when a Supporter is played. `EffectCtx.playedSupporterThisTurn(name: string): boolean`. `viewFor` exposes it on `you`, and the determinizer copies it.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** (`gym-markers.test.ts`, with registry-free fixture cards like `markers.test.ts`):
  - `increaseOutgoing` 120 with `turns = 2` still applies on the owner's next turn (turn + 2) and has expired by turn + 3.
  - `increaseOutgoing` applies before Weakness: (30 + 20) × 2 = 100.
  - `preventDamage` makes an attack do 0, and does nothing against `ignoreDefenderEffects`.
  - `attackCostMore` makes a 1-Energy attack unavailable with 1 Energy and available with 2, and it expires with the marker.
  - `retreatCostMore` raises the retreat cost by 1, and a Pokémon with 1 Energy then can't retreat.
  - `ignoreResistance`: a defender with Resistance takes full damage, while its Weakness still doubles.
  - `addMarker` removes a marker that expired earlier, and keeps one that hasn't.
  - Playing a Supporter sets `supporterPlayed`; `playedSupporterThisTurn` is true that turn and false the next.
  - Determinizer round trip: a slot with a new-kind marker and a player with `supporterPlayed` survive `viewFor` → determinize unchanged.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run `npx vitest run --project engine --project bots`. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement. In `view.ts` and `determinize.ts` copy the new fields the way `markers` and `becameActiveTurn` are copied.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run `pnpm check`. Expected: green.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Commit `feat(engine): more timed markers, Resistance option, supporter memory`.

### Task 4: Engine B — knockout hooks, team retreat cost, self-discard, forced evolve Abilities

**Files:**

- Modify: `packages/engine/src/cards.ts`, `combat.ts`, `energy.ts`, `effects.ts`, `actions.ts`, `index.ts`
- Test: `packages/engine/test/gym-hooks.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**

- Produces on `CardScript`:
  - `survivesKnockout?(ctx: EffectCtx, holder: SlotRef): boolean`: `checkKnockouts` calls it for a Pokémon whose damage has reached its HP **only when the Knock Out comes from damage by an attack** (an attack is resolving and the Pokémon was damaged by it). If it returns true, the Pokémon survives with `damage = maxHp - 10`.
  - `afterKnockout?(ctx: EffectCtx, e: { holder: SlotRef; knocked: SlotRef; attacker: SlotRef }): void`: after an attack Knocks Out a Pokémon, called once for each Pokémon of the Knocked Out Pokémon's owner that has the hook, including the Knocked Out one itself, while it is still in play and before Prizes are taken.
  - `modifyAllRetreatCosts?(q: { state; holder: SlotRef; slot: SlotRef; cost: number; registry }): number`: applied for every Pokémon in play of the holder's owner, from every Pokémon of that owner in play.
  - `onEvolveFromHand` gains `mandatory?: boolean`; when true the engine skips the "Use Ability?" question and runs it.
- Produces on `EffectCtx`: `discardSlot(ref: SlotRef): void`, which moves the Pokémon, its evolution cards, Energy and Tool to its owner's discard pile and removes the slot (an Active slot becomes empty). No Prize is taken.
- After an Ability resolves, the engine runs the same Knock Out and promotion step it runs after an attack (damage counters from an Ability can Knock Out, and `discardSlot` can empty the Active Spot).

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** (`gym-hooks.test.ts`):
  - `survivesKnockout`: with the hook returning true, a Pokémon hit for lethal damage survives with 10 HP left; the same Pokémon KO'd by Poison or Burn in Pokémon Checkup is Knocked Out; with the hook returning false, it is Knocked Out.
  - `afterKnockout`:
    - the Knocked Out Active's own hook runs, and a Benched ally's hook runs once with `knocked` pointing at the Active;
    - neither runs for a Knock Out by Poison;
    - the opponent's Pokémon don't get the callback.
  - `modifyAllRetreatCosts` from a Benched Pokémon makes the Active's retreat cost 0, and does not affect the opponent's Pokémon.
  - `mandatory` evolve Ability runs without a prompt, while a non-mandatory one still asks.
  - `discardSlot`: a Benched Pokémon with Energy and a Tool goes to the discard pile, and the Bench closes up; discarding the Active with a Bench prompts a promotion; discarding the Active with an empty Bench ends the game as a loss for that player.
  - An Ability that discards the Active Pokémon triggers promotion after it resolves, with no stuck prompt.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run `npx vitest run --project engine`. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement the interfaces above. `checkKnockouts` must know whether a Knock Out came from attack damage; reuse the attack bookkeeping `combat.ts` already keeps (`currentAttack`).
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run `pnpm check`. Expected: green.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Commit `feat(engine): knockout hooks, team retreat cost, discardSlot, forced evolve Abilities`.
